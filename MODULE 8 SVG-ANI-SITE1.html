Example 03

Self-Drawing Line

This example uses stroke-dasharray and stroke-dashoffset to create the effect of a line drawing itself when the page loads. The animation stays in its final drawn position with animation-fill-mode: forwards.

Example 04

Fade-Up Entrance

This example combines opacity and transform: translateY() inside one keyframe animation. A short delay makes the SVG enter after the page begins loading.
